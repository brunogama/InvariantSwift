## Decisions Made

**1. OSAllocatedUnfairLock for MetricsInterceptor**
- Rationale: Swift 6 concurrency-safe, lower overhead than Actor for simple counters
- Pattern: `OSAllocatedUnfairLock<Metrics>` with `withLock` for atomic updates
- Alternative considered: Actor (rejected due to async overhead for simple increments)

**2. Default no-op implementations in protocol extension**
- Rationale: Interceptors only need to implement hooks they care about
- Pattern: Empty implementations for onGenerate, onShrink, onPropertyEvaluated
- Benefit: Minimal boilerplate for focused interceptors (e.g., MetricsInterceptor only needs onGenerate)

**3. Type-erased interceptor array support**
- Rationale: Enable heterogeneous interceptor chaining
- Pattern: `withInterceptors(_ interceptors: [any GeneratorInterceptor])`
- Benefit: Combine different interceptor types in single chain

**4. Convenience methods for common cases**
- Rationale: Reduce boilerplate for debugging and metrics
- Pattern: `logged()` and `withMetrics()` methods
- Benefit: `Gen.int.logged()` instead of `Gen.int.withInterceptor(LoggingInterceptor())`
